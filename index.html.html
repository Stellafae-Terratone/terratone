🎬 Earth Video Sonification

Choose a NASA Earth video saved on your computer. The page listens to the colours inside the video and turns them into notes.

Choose a video to begin.

How the sound works

  • Warm colours (red, orange) = higher notes
  • Cool colours (blue) = lower notes
  • Brighter picture = louder sound
  • Lots of red in the picture = a second, higher note joins in
  • Black space = silence

Colour reading will appear here while the video plays.

Describe how the sound works in words

The page looks at the video about five times every second. It ignores the black background of space and measures the colours of the Earth. If the Earth looks warmer (more red and orange), it plays a higher note. If it looks cooler (more blue), it plays a lower note. A brighter picture is louder. When a lot of red appears, a second note plays above the first. All notes come from a pleasant five-note scale, so the result is musical.

Video: NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, or the NASA Earth Information Center (earth.gov). The sound is generated live from the colours in the video.
